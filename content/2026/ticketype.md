I called my set of fonts Ticketype, a combination of the words ticket and type. The inspiration comes from the fact that my site is peckhamplex, and my font contains the characteristic elements of movie tickets, so it is simple and clear. I think people can directly relate to the relaxed atmosphere brought by movie tickets and theaters when using this font.
