This typeface is a display-focused design inspired by the architecture and infrastructure of Peckham Rye Station. It draws heavily on structural and industrial elements found at the site, such as brickwork, rail tracks and grid systems. The alphabet is built using consistent proportions and widths to create a cohesive system, while variations in internal structure add character and visual interest. The influences from the station are reflected in the bold letterforms, strong vertical strokes, and repeated internal details that give the typeface a constructed, architectural feel.
