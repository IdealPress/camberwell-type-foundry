WINEDOWN. A font inspired by the pub weatherspoons, a place where all people can go, drink, eat and 'wind down'.Initially my project was centered around utensils, but I had a change of heart because that's not what people think about when they go to spoons, they think of having a drink,so my type font is made out of wine glasses. The wine glass is drawn messily, to convey the feeling after a night of spoons, disorientated, buzzed and fuzzy which I think was depicted well, reflecting weatherspoons in the best way. WINEDOWN is best suited for titles and display purposes.
