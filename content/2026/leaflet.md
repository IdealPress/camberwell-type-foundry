I chose Walworth Garden as the design location and used it as inspiration to create the typeface Leaflet. During the design process, I observed the growth of plants and the shape of leaves in the garden, and incorporated leaf elements into the structure of the typeface, so that the outline of the characters simulates the growth shape of tree branches, allowing viewers to naturally associate the typeface with the garden and plant environment when reading it.
