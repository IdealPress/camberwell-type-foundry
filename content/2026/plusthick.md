PLUSTHICK is a typeface for cheap things. Inspired by Khan's Bargains, it reflects tightly packed objects, crowded shelves, and endless choices ' you never know what you will find, but you can always find what you need. Dense, heavy letterforms mirror tight spaces, visual overload, and everyday abundance. Cheap, plastic, and a little random. And who says cheap things can't be good?
