Inspired by the nature surrounding St. Giles Church and included in the churchyard, this font adds a whimsy and creative twist to the usually structured and rigid architecture of the building. the branches in the churchyard twist to provide an enchanting atmosphere before stepping into the Church. The Churchyard is also home to many squirrels that see it is as a habitat and safe place from the bustling streets of London.
