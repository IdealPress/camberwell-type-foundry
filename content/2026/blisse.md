Blisse is a typeface that captures the essence of Bussey Building's bustling social environment. It mainly focuses on the retro atmosphere of the area, taking inspiration and form from the patterns of rips and markings on the alleyway walls. The design focuses on notions of self-expression found in the area left by different groups and the existence of a creative community and how this affects the social crowd of Peckham.
