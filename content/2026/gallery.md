Gallery is a display typeface developed from observations of the South London Gallery.
The letterforms are based on the building's architectural features, including arches, symmetry, and block-like stone structures.
After the design process, the typeface was found to be most suitable for use in titles and promotional materials.
Its structured and square forms work well for cultural and community events, such as small-scale festivals or public activities.
