Denmark Hill Node is a site-specific display typeface developed in response to the visual language of Denmark Hill station. The typeface is constructed using a modular grid system, where letterforms are built from connected nodes and linear segments, referencing transport networks, wayfinding systems, and infrastructural signage. The design explores rhythm, spacing, and structure, translating architectural and navigational elements into typographic form. Denmark Hill Node is intended for large-scale display, signage, and experimental applications where structure and place are central.
