My font draws inspiration from the visual aspect of Theatre Peckham . There were Ghana Kente print pillows in the cafe of the Theatre, so I looked at how I can use in the patterns within the pillow to create individual letters.  Theatre Peckham like to represent the diverse community of Peckham and within the theatre 69% of the trustees are black, asian or ethnically diverse which shows how they like to create an inclusive space.
