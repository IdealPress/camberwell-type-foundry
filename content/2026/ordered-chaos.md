This font named "Ordered Chaos" deeply deconstructs the postmodern architectural language of 95 Peckham Rd. The design inspiration comes from Peter Barber's blocky reorganization of the brick facade. By transforming the irregular window arrangement into the negative space of the font, it presents a kind of "ordered chaos". The stroke structure imitates the geometric misalignment and heavy texture of architecture, and uses modular logic to balance artistic expression and readability, aiming to explore the symbiotic relationship between urban space and textual form.
