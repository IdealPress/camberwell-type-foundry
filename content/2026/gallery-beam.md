My Typeface focuses on the functional aspect of the gallery. When I
thought about how artworks are lit in the space, I became interested in the
idea of using light as the main design element. A rectangle could represent
the artwork, while the shapes of spotlight beams could interact with it to
form letter structures. This creates a typeface that reflects both the display
environment and the role of lighting in shaping the viewer's experience.
