Hactiff is a type face based on Sky Shopping City on Rye Lane, Peckham, a small shopping centre that was built in the late 1970s - early 80s. Hactiff is inspired by the slab serif fronts of old newspapers and market stalls and the graffiti that emerges from Peckham's diverse, artistic and energetic spirit. The serfs In Hactiff are characterful and fun, with each capital letter having its own personality. The lowercase set is more subdued, allowing for Hactiff to be used as both a display font and a text font if required.
