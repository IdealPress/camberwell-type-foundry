'Crocket' is inspired by the architectural details found within the windows and arches of St Giles Church in Camberwell. A key feature within this Church is a Crocket, which is a decorative motif commonly used within Gothic architecture, usually in stylised carvings of leaves, buds or flowers. Inspired by this element, each character in the typeface has a Crocket embedded into it. Also influenced by the nature of the arches and Gothic elements, the letterforms portray tall and thin characteristics and are composed of carefully placed serifs, reflecting the precision of carved stonework.
