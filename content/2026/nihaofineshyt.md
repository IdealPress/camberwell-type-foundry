inspired by the visual language of burgerking which is located in a rich history street. Arcadia draws from the contrast between its surrounding heritage and contemporary commercial presence. The typeface is based on classical English cursive letterforms, reinterpreted through the addition of modern elements such as simplified structures, enhanced clarity, and a sense of lightness. By combining traditional flowing strokes with clean geometry, Arcadia reflects the coexistence of history and modernity within the urban environment. The typeface translates this spatial and cultural contrast into a contemporary visual language, integrating echoes of the past into a clean, modern context.
