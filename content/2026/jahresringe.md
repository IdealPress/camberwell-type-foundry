Jahresringe is a bold display typeface based around tree rings.
It is  inspired by the interaction between humans and nature and showcases how they can thrive alongside each other without destroying one another.
It features a bold uppercase which is designed to represent a tree stump. The lowercase and symbols are different thicknesses to illustrate the different rings within the tree and showcase the impact that humans have on their environment.
The detailed texture makes it best used in large sizes, for titles and catching attention.
