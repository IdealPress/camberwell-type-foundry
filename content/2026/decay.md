Decay, inspired by the St Giles church, decay showcases the power of time and decay through the chipping away and deterioration of the letters, just like the architecture of and around the church. The forms are inspired by the slow processes of time, erosion, and decay, capturing the way carved surfaces soften, fracture, and transform over time. In its strongest and weakest forms, the typeface showcases that no matter how long it has been or what the damage is, the church and its surrounding architecure stand tall, showcasing the endurance, deterioration, and memory of the site.
