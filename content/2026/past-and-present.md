For my typeface I explored history, tradition and modern society and created a typeface that represented both styles into one. grove house tavern kept its traditional style in a modern society and I wanted to explore this in my typeface by combining both elements visually together. I wanted to equally represent old and new together to homage its history and style, as grove house tavern feels stuck in time whilst everything else has moved on and been modernised. This is a display typeface that would be mainly used for titles.
