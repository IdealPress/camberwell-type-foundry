Graveyard is a font inspired by the location of Nunhead Cemetery and its gothic architecture. The withered and tattered graves alongside the overgrown and unkempt plants that surround death produce an unearthly and spectral sensation when walking along its path, and as you come face to face with your immortality you realize all paths must come to a close. The font was created to capture the decay of the cemetery throughout the years; the graves out of reach due to the vast aging of nature. Lineweight flows from thick to thin, and the sharpness captures that harsh truth of our inevitable end.
